Earnora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28 · Contact: earnora.app@gmail.com

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Earnora mobile app ("App") processes data. By default, data is stored locally on your device.

2. Data processed

On device we may store: hourly rate and currency, work schedule, goal amounts, goals and progress, calculation history, onboarding flag, premium/ad-free status. We do not require name, passport data, or contacts for basic calculator use.

3. Storage

Core app data is stored locally (device storage). We do not sync your goals/calculations to our own server; the App has no mandatory cloud account.

4. Purchases

Payments for tips/premium are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive or store your card number.

5. Ads

The free version uses Google AdMob and its consent platform. These services may process IP address and approximate location, device or app-scoped identifiers, ads shown and interactions, app interactions, crash logs, performance information and other diagnostic data for advertising, analytics and SDK operation. Earnora requests non-personalized ads and does not request permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Advertising starts only when the consent SDK permits it. Where required, a consent form and privacy choices are available in the app. After activating Premium, in-app ads stop. Google’s policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Sharing may occur with app stores for purchases, ad partners via SDKs if enabled, or when required by law.

7. Retention

Local goals and calculation history remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear app data or uninstall. App stores and advertising providers retain data under their own policies. For questions or privacy requests, contact earnora.app@gmail.com.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by local law).

9. Changes

We may update this Policy. The latest version is also available in the App under More → Privacy policy.

10. Support and contact

For help with Earnora, purchases, restoration or privacy, email the developer. Include your app version and a description of the issue. Do not send payment card details or passwords.
